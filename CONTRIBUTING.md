# Contributing to Zetheta-Market-Regime-Multi-Asset-Analysis-Framework

Thank you for your interest in contributing! Whether it's reporting a bug, suggesting a new feature, or optimizing a regime-detection model, contributions are welcome.

1. **Fork the Repository** on GitHub (`taruncal/Zetheta-Market-Regime-Multi-Asset-Analysis-Framework`).
2. **Clone Your Fork** to your local machine:
   ```bash
